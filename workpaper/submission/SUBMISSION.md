## Review path

Overview → Evidence → N1 → D28 printed basis → D11 supplemental basis → Findings → Collaboration and role switching → Versions / Replay / What-ifs → Filing.

The recorded hosted workspace completes an N1 adjustment through exact-package approvals, simulated federal acceptance and California rejection, and an amendment that preserves the parent. A fresh visitor receives a separate sandbox and can repeat the sequence. Follow [TRANSCRIPT.md](TRANSCRIPT.md) for the detailed click path.

## Scope

This prototype demonstrates evidence, review, permissions, and preserved decisions around a supplied fictional tax packet. Four finding groups and the Brightpath 260-lot positive control are computed independently. Broader specialist tax work remains disclosed in the approach and error register. Full-return readiness stays blocked, tax effects are labeled estimates, and no real return is filed.

[Hosted validation](HOSTED_VALIDATION.md) records the completed workflow and export integrity checks. [Local validation](VALIDATION.md) records tests and build checks. Nothing has been emailed or submitted to the hiring team by the application or coding agent.
